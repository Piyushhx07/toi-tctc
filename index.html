Why it matters

Small actions create lasting impact

We focus on things a single community can actually change — the river behind the school, the vacant lot on the corner, the recycling habits of one neighbourhood.

Clean Water Access

Chapters monitor local water quality and organize cleanup drives along rivers, canals, and community water sources.

Urban Green Cover

We plant and maintain trees on unused public land, tracked and cared for by the nearest community chapter.

Waste & Recycling

Practical workshops on sorting, composting, and reducing household waste, run by trained volunteers.

Everyday Action

Meetups happen monthly, not once a year — consistent momentum matters more than a single big event.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Is there any cost to register?

No. Registration and all listed activities are completely free. The initiative is community-funded, not subscription-based.

What happens after I submit the form?

You'll see a confirmation screen and be redirected to the TCTC platform. A chapter coordinator will also reach out within a few days.

Do I need any prior experience?

Not at all. Most members start with a single cleanup drive or workshop and go from there. Everyone is welcome.

Can I register a school or organization?

Yes — mention that in the form's message field and a coordinator will follow up about group or institutional registration.

Is my data safe?

Absolutely. Your information is stored securely in our system and is never shared with third parties. We only use it to connect you with your local chapter.
